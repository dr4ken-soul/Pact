# Pact Market Introduction

## Copy-ready post

Meet Pact: a Bitcoin Cash payment prototype for AI agents. Set an approved recipient, a per-payment cap, and an expiry. An agent can propose a payment, but Pact asks the wallet owner to review and sign it through WizardConnect, so the agent never gets the keys. Pact is built to make agent-initiated BCH payments more controlled and transparent. Its current spending rules are app-level checks, and every transaction still needs the owner's approval.
